I'm Miika Nissi, and I specialize in providing reliable, high-quality Odoo ERP
development and consulting. With attention to detail, I offer practical solutions
tailored to your specific business needs. Let's work together to streamline your
operations and maximize your efficiency with Odoo.

## Services

- **Odoo customization and development** — Need specific functionalities or workflows
  tailored to your business processes? As a seasoned Odoo developer, I build reliable,
  high-quality modules and modifications that work seamlessly with your existing
  systems. Any change to the system, if not done correctly, can cause issues down the
  line, so I pay close attention to get it right the first time.

- **Business process consulting** — Before writing any code, it's worth asking whether
  the process itself is the right one. I help you map how your business actually runs
  and where Odoo's standard flows can do the work without custom development at all.

- **Implementation** — I guide businesses through the entire Odoo implementation
  process, from initial planning to deployment, ensuring a smooth transition. Whether
  you're planning on self-hosting Odoo or using a cloud hosting platform, I can share my
  knowledge and expertise to help you make the right decision.

- **Migrations and upgrades** — Stay up to date with the latest Odoo features and
  enhancements. I handle migrations and upgrades with care, ensuring minimal downtime
  and preserving data integrity. I've migrated Odoo databases across versions 8
  through 20.

- **Integrations and APIs** — Integrate Odoo with your existing systems and third-party
  applications to streamline operations.

## How an engagement works

Every engagement starts with a conversation about what you're trying to solve, not what
module you think you need. From there I scope the work, give you a clear estimate, and
check in as I go rather than disappearing until the end. I don't charge for a solution
until it's fully tested and approved.

## Why a one-person studio

Working with one developer means one person who knows your whole system, no hand-offs
between account managers and engineers, and direct communication throughout. It also
means I'm selective about the projects I take on, so I can give each one the attention
it needs.
