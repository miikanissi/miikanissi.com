This site sets no cookies and runs no JavaScript. There are no analytics, ads, tracking
pixels or embedded widgets. The fonts and nearly all images are served from this site's
own server, so reading a page doesn't send your visit to Google or anyone else.

## Server logs

The web server keeps standard access logs: your IP address, your browser's user agent,
the page you asked for and the time. I use them only to fix problems and keep the server
secure. They aren't shared or analysed, and they're deleted when the logs rotate.

## Email

If you email me, I keep your message and address so I can reply. I won't add you to a
mailing list or share your address with anyone.

## Other sites

One post, [Modus Themes for Neovim](/blog/modus-themes-for-neovim/), shows status badges
and a preview image that load from GitHub and shields.io. Opening that post sends a
request to those services, and their privacy policies apply. The same goes for any site
you reach by following a link from here.
